FAQ

Learning & Content

How do I start a lesson?
Navigate to your desired subject using the menu or search bar, then tap on a lesson card to begin.
What subjects are available?
The app currently supports Science, Geography, English, Math, and Chemistry, with more to come.
How do I add or submit flashcards or notes?
Use the "Add flashcard" or "Notes" section available within each subject tab.

Progress Tracking

Where can I view my progress?
Visit the "Analysis" page or check the progress bars within each subject tile.
What does the percentage next to a subject mean?
It shows your completion rate or performance in that subject.

Notes & Collaboration

Can I take notes during lessons?
Yes, head to the "Notes" section to write and save your thoughts or answers.
Can I share notes with others?
This feature may be part of future releases. For now, notes are saved locally to your account.

Leaderboard & Motivation

How does the leaderboard work?
The leaderboard ranks users based on lesson completion and quiz performance.
How can I improve my rank?
Focus on finishing lessons and scoring well on quizzes.

Notifications & Updates

What are the "Updates" and "Missing" tabs for?
They show new content uploads, score updates, and any missed or pending files/notes.
